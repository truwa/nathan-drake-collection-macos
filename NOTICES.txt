Nathan Drake Collection for Mac
================================

This app starts the BilalReloaded PC port of Uncharted: The Nathan Drake
Collection on Apple Silicon, through Rosetta 2 and KosmicKrisp. It is not a
Sony, Naughty Dog or Bluepoint product. The games are not included. You choose
the folder that contains your own decrypted dump (eboot.bin, sce_sys, u1data,
u2data, u3data) and, if you dumped it separately, the 1.02 update folder.
